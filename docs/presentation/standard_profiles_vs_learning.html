What we don't know yet

Four questions two days can't answer

Profile or scope?
The measured data also holds users the official profiles don't cover. How much of the overestimate is the profile itself?
Can it handle extreme winters?
One of our AI methods cannot go colder than the coldest day it has seen: it missed the real cold spells by 7–9%.
Does it work for electricity?
Official standard profiles exist for electricity too. Would the same test carry over?
Can two models help each other?
The WPG heat model is physics-based: buildings and behaviour. Ours learns from measured data. Can they work together?
